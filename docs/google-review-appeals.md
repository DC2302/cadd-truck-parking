# Google review removal — report & appeal pack

For the two text-free 1-star ratings on the CADD Truck Parking profile
(Cole Gregory, Beto Carrera — both posted ~2023).

**Before you send anything:** every factual claim below must be one you have
actually checked. Anything in `[BRACKETS]` is a blank only you can fill. If you
haven't verified it, delete the sentence — don't guess. A single claim Google
can poke a hole in sinks the whole appeal, and an unverifiable statement is
worse than a shorter, airtight one.

---

## Step 1 — Report each review

Profile → the review → **⋮** → **Report review**.

Choose **"Fake engagement"** (some menus word it "Spam or fake content" /
"Content not based on a real experience"). That is the policy these violate:
a rating from someone with no experience of the business.

Do **not** pick "Off-topic" or "Offensive" — there's no text, so those don't
apply and a wrong category gets a fast denial.

Then track status in the Reviews Management Tool:
https://support.google.com/business/workflow/9945796

---

## Step 2 — The appeal (one per review, use it deliberately)

You get **ONE appeal per review**, decided in ~5 business days. After you submit
it, Google shows a link to add supporting evidence that **expires 60 minutes
later** — so have Step 3 ready in a file before you start.

## Step 3 — Supporting evidence (the 60-minute form)

Have this written and your files saved **before** you appeal.

**What to write:**

> Our lot is access-controlled and every space is sold under a named, signed
> parking agreement, so our records show exactly who has used the facility.
>
> Records searched: [LIST THEM — e.g. reservation records, Square/Zelle/Cash App
> payment history, signed rental agreements, gate-access log]
> Period searched: [DATES]
> Result: no match for either name; no call, email, or written complaint on file.
>
> Both ratings are text-free, were posted within [X weeks/months] of each other,
> and both remain unexplained after public owner replies requesting details.
> Every other review on our profile describes an actual visit.

**What to attach (only what you actually have):**
- A screenshot of a search of your customer/payment records for each name,
  showing zero results
- A screenshot of your public reply to each review, with the date
- Anything showing the two postings clustered in time

**Do not attach** customer lists, other people's personal information, or
payment details. Show the *search returning nothing*, not your whole database.

---

## What NOT to say

- Don't call them "fake reviews" emotionally or say a competitor did it unless
  you have actual proof — an unsupported accusation weakens a strong case.
- Don't threaten legal action in these forms. It doesn't speed anything up.
- Don't mention lost revenue or how unfair it is. Reviewers act on policy
  violations, not hardship.
- Don't say "we have no record" if you haven't actually run the search. Run it,
  then say which systems you searched.
