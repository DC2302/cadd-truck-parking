<p>"24/7 truck parking" gets typed into a phone a lot in the Permian — usually at 11 p.m. by somebody whose clock just ran out. The problem is that half the places that answer to that phrase mean "there's a lot and nobody will run you off," not "you can get in, get a shower, and sleep with the doors unlocked." Here's what 24/7 should mean, and what it means at CADD.</p>

<h2>24/7 means the gate opens at 3 a.m.</h2>

<p>Round-the-clock access is the whole point. At CADD Truck Parking the gates are open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, with cameras running the entire time. You reserve online, you get your assigned space number, and you pull in whenever the day ends — no office hours, no waiting on a call-back to get a code. The restroom keypad code comes through as soon as your payment is received, and IronHauler members carry their own key to the showers.</p>

<h2>"Secure" means fenced, gated, watched — and assigned</h2>

<p>Plenty of yards are open ground with a sign. Ours is fully fenced with gated entry points and 24/7 camera coverage across the lot. Every space is numbered and assigned to you, which matters more than it sounds: nobody parks in your spot while you're on a run, nobody blocks you in, and there's no jockeying for a place to shut down at midnight. Your personal vehicle can sit in your assigned space too, or in the personal-vehicle parking by the pre-trip station and the exit gate.</p>
